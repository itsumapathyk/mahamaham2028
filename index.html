About Mahamaham

Mahamaham is a major Hindu festival held approximately once every 12 years at the Mahamaham Tank in Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu. It is associated with the Tamil month of Maasi, the Magha nakshatra and auspicious planetary alignments.

🕉️

Sacred Bath

Devotees take a ceremonial dip in the Mahamaham Tank, seeking spiritual purification and blessings.

🛕

Temple Town

Kumbakonam is renowned for its historic temples, traditional crafts, and rich Tamil cultural heritage.

👥

Mass Gathering

The festival draws devotees from across India and abroad, with extensive arrangements for crowd movement and safety.

Pilgrim Information

Visitor Guide

🧭

Plan Early

Book transport and accommodation well in advance. Expect exceptionally high demand during the festival period.

🚉

Use Public Transport

Prefer trains and authorised shuttle services. Follow police and volunteer guidance for entry and exit routes.

🩺

Health & Safety

Carry water, medicines and identification. Follow crowd-control instructions, especially near the tank and temple areas.

🎒

Travel Light

Avoid large bags and valuables. Use designated cloakrooms and parking areas where available.

📱

Stay Informed

Save emergency contacts and follow official announcements for route changes, timings and weather advisories.

🙏

Respect Traditions

Dress modestly, maintain queue discipline and help preserve the sanctity of temple and tank premises.

Getting There

Travel & Stay

🚆

By Rail

Kumbakonam Railway Station is the nearest major railhead. Special trains and additional services may be announced closer to the festival.

🚌

By Road

Kumbakonam is well connected by Tamil Nadu State Transport and private buses from Chennai, Trichy, Thanjavur and nearby towns.

✈️

By Air

Tiruchirappalli International Airport is a convenient air gateway, followed by road or rail travel to Kumbakonam.
